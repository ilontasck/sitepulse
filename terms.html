5. Audit results

Audit reports are produced by automated analysis only. They are intended as a technical starting point, not a comprehensive professional assessment. The following limitations apply:

  • Automated checks have inherent limitations. Automated tools cannot detect all categories of quality issue and may produce false positives or miss genuine problems.
  • Scores are indicative, not definitive. Scoring models are based on heuristics and do not constitute a certified accessibility, SEO, or security audit.
  • A single point-in-time snapshot. Results reflect the state of the website at the moment of the audit. Websites change; results may become outdated immediately.
  • Rendered audit is optional and resource-limited. If the rendered (Chromium-based) audit is unavailable or times out, the service falls back to an HTML-only analysis. The report will note which mode was used.
  • The service does not store the HTML of audited pages. Only derived scores and findings are retained.

Results should not be used as the sole basis for commercial, legal, or compliance decisions without independent expert review.
